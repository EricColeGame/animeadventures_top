## What Makes a Unit the Best in Anime Adventures?

Rarity is a starting point, not an answer. A Secret-tier unit that costs too much to place or only hits one target can underperform a well-built Mythical sitting in the same slot. Any anime adventures best units ranking worth reading weighs five practical factors before it looks at border color.

| Factor | What to Look For | Why It Decides Runs | Red Flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Placement cost | Affordable by wave 3–5 | A carry you can't afford is a carry you don't have | Needs 10+ waves of income before it lands |
| Damage shape | AoE for waves, single-target for bosses | Trash waves and boss waves punish different builds | Pure single-target in an Infinite run |
| Upgrade curve | Strong gains from early levels | Snowballs before enemies scale | Flat scaling that stalls past mid-game |
| Range and coverage | Long reach from safe tiles | Fewer wasted placements, more path coverage | Tiny range that forces awkward spots |
| Utility | Stuns, slows, buffs, debuffs | Utility multiplies your whole team's damage | A "carry" that contributes nothing else |

The other half of the equation is your account, not the unit. A maxed, well-traited damage dealer feels completely different from the same unit at level one with no upgrades. Player experience is consistent on this point: investment matters as much as the pull itself, so judge units by how they perform *after* you can realistically build them.

## Best Units in Anime Adventures by Role

Every strong roster is built around roles, not a pile of the highest rarities. If you only remember one thing from this guide, make it this table — it tells you what your squad is missing before you spend a single gem.

| Role | Job in the Squad | What to Prioritize | Build Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| AoE damage dealer | Clears entire waves of enemies | Wide splash, fast attack speed | Highest |
| Single-target boss killer | Melts high-HP raid and Legend bosses | Burst damage, high scaling | High |
| Farm / income | Generates cash every wave | Cost efficiency, early placement | High (early game) |
| Support / buffer | Boosts team damage, slows, stuns | Team-wide effects, low cost | Medium |

A balanced squad of four usually looks like two damage dealers, one income unit, and one support — then you swap the support for a second boss killer when you queue into raids. Chasing four Secret carries with no income and no utility is the single most common roster mistake in the game.

### Farm and Support Units That Pay for Themselves

Damage wins fights; economy wins *runs*. Income units fund your carries, and support units multiply damage you already have. Neither shows up in flashy highlight clips, which is why so many players underrate them.

| Archetype | What It Does | What to Look For | When to Build |
|---|---|---|---|
| Income unit | Generates cash each wave | Scales with upgrades, cheap to place | Wave 1–3, always |
| Buffer | Raises team damage output | Percentage-based boosts, wide radius | Before your first boss wave |
| Crowd control | Slows or stuns enemies | AoE effects, short cooldown | Infinite and long stages |
| Hybrid support | Damages and buffs | Dual effects, low cost | Flexible filler slot |

- **Build income first.** One or two economy units early means your carries arrive several waves sooner.
- **Stack buffs with your best damage dealer.** Buffs multiply existing damage, so they get stronger as your carry gets stronger.
- **Don't over-invest in support.** Support units usually want upgrades and a trait, not a full max-level commitment.
- **Swap, don't sell.** Keep a support in reserve for Infinite runs even if you run pure damage in story mode.

## Matching the Best Units to Each Game Mode

The anime adventures best units for Infinite mode are not automatically the best for story farming. Each mode asks a different question, and the answer changes your ideal squad shape.

| Mode | What It Demands | Ideal Squad Shape | Quick Tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Story | Fast clears, low placement cost | 2 AoE + 1 income + 1 support | Speed matters more than raw damage |
| Infinite | Sustained AoE plus crowd control | 2 AoE + 1 CC + 1 buffer | Control buys the time your damage needs |
| Raids | Heavy single-target burst | 2 boss killers + 2 buffers | Time your burst windows together |
| Legend stages | Balanced damage and utility | 2 damage + 1 support + 1 flex | Bring one CC unit as insurance |
| Event farming | Fast, repeatable clears | Fast AoE + income | Optimize for clear speed, not safety |

If you're unsure where to start, build one AoE carry and one income unit to a high level before chasing anything else. That pair alone unlocks most early and mid-game content and gives you a stable foundation to build around.

## How to Get, Evolve, and Trait Your Top Units

Knowing which anime adventures best units to chase is only half the job — the other half is acquiring and building them efficiently.

**Acquisition.** Most top units come from summon banners using premium currency, while others arrive through evolution items, event rewards, or trading. Banners rotate, so patience is genuinely a strategy: hoarding currency for a banner featuring a unit you actually need beats spreading pulls across every release.

**Evolution.** Many units have an evolved form that changes their stats and sometimes their role entirely. Before you invest heavily in a unit, check whether an evolution exists — evolving first prevents wasted upgrade materials.

**Traits.** Traits are where good units become great ones. Match the trait to the job rather than chasing a single "best" option.

| Trait Category | Best Applied To | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|
| Raw damage boosts | Main damage dealers | Multiplies everything else you've built |
| Attack speed | AoE wave clearers | More hits means more total damage |
| Range increases | Short-range carries | Opens safer, more efficient placements |
| Cost reduction | Income and support units | Gets your engine running earlier |

**Upgrade order.** Spend resources in this sequence and you'll rarely feel stuck:

| Step | Action | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Level one AoE carry to a strong breakpoint | Unlocks most story content |
| 2 | Build one income unit | Funds everything that follows |
| 3 | Add a support or CC unit | Handles Infinite and longer stages |
| 4 | Add a single-target boss killer | Covers raids and Legend bosses |
| 5 | Trait and evolve your carry | Biggest single power spike per resource |
| 6 | Round out with hybrids and dupes | Fills gaps for event and niche modes |

## FAQ

**What are the anime adventures best units for beginners?**
Start with a single strong AoE damage dealer and one income unit rather than chasing Secret-rarity pulls. That combination clears story stages, funds your upgrades, and teaches you placement timing before you invest in expensive carries.

**Do I need Secret units to clear Infinite mode?**
No. Well-built Mythical units with good traits, plus a crowd-control support, can carry you surprisingly deep. Secret carries simply raise your ceiling and reduce how perfect your placement has to be.

**Should I trade for the anime adventures best units or summon them?**
It depends on your patience and currency. Summoning is cheaper on average but random; trading gets you a specific unit faster if you have desirable duplicates to offer. Never trade away a unit you're actively using to chase a tier-list upgrade.

**How often does the unit meta change?**
Typically whenever a major balance patch or a new banner lands. Roles like "AoE carry" and "income unit" never go out of style, so build around roles and you'll adapt to changes far faster than players who chase individual units.
