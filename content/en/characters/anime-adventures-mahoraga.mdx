## Reading the Kit: What Mahoraga Actually Does

Community reports consistently describe Mahoraga as a "commit and hold" unit. It is not a fire-and-forget tower you drop in a corner and forget about. It wants a stable lane, a steady cash flow, and a few seconds of breathing room before the heavy enemies arrive.

That design comes straight from its source material. In Jujutsu Kaisen, Mahoraga is the Divine General of the Ten Shadows technique — a summoned being that adapts to whatever it fights. If you want to see that concept in motion before you build around it, streaming services such as [Crunchyroll's official anime library](https://www.crunchyroll.com/) carry the series that inspired the unit.

### Upgrade Order That Actually Works

Most player experience points to the same sequence:

1. **Level the unit first.** Raw levels are usually the cheapest reliable power gain.
2. **Then chase placement efficiency.** Move it before you pour more cash into it.
3. **Then invest in traits.** Traits are the expensive layer — do not rush them on a unit you have not learned to position.
4. **Then evolution materials.** These raise the ceiling, but they are wasted if the fundamentals are shaky.

## Builds, Placement, and Upgrade Priorities

Placement is where Mahoraga earns or loses its slot. The unit wants enemies to walk into it, bunch up, and stay in range. That means bends, chokepoints, and crossroads beat open straightaways almost every time.

| Situation | Placement approach | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long straight lane | Sit near a bend where enemies cluster | Maximizes time-on-target per wave |
| Split-path map | Anchor the mid-map crossroads | Covers both lanes with one investment |
| Boss rush | Place slightly behind the front line | Survives longer, keeps dealing damage |
| Infinite mode, late waves | Behind a tank or farm line | Protects a very expensive unit |
| Challenge maps with fast enemies | Near the spawn-adjacent choke | Prevents leaks before they snowball |

Bullet-point checklist before you commit:

- Confirm your economy can fund at least two early upgrades.
- Make sure a blocker or staller sits in front of it.
- Check that the lane does not split immediately after your placement point.
- Decide in advance whether this unit is your boss answer or your wave answer — not both.

### Common Mistakes That Sink Runs

- **Placing it first.** A damage unit with no economy behind it is a decoration.
- **Spreading upgrades thin.** Two strong units beat five mediocre ones.
- **Ignoring the lane shape.** Range does not help if enemies never enter it.
- **Bringing it to the wrong mode.** It shines in endurance fights, not quick clears.
- **Chasing traits too early.** Traits amplify a good setup; they do not fix a bad one.
